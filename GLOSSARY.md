# AquaSci 1 Scheduler

A shared, one-time scheduling tool for the AquaSci 1 outreach and the class running it. These words are fixed so "event" never means two things.

## Language

**Event**:
One scheduling round — a date poll for the outreach, a meeting, a committee sync, or a dry run. Each has its own candidate days, hours, and replies.
_Avoid_: Poll, round, survey

**Activity**:
The AquaSci 1 educational outreach for elementary students and younger children — the thing the dates are being picked for.
_Avoid_: Event, project

**Classmate**:
One of the 25 students organizing the activity. Classmates pick their name and supply their own available times.
_Avoid_: Participant, member, user, respondent

**PM (Project Manager)**:
The classmate who owns the schedule and edits events, candidate days, and the timeline in the Supabase dashboard.
_Avoid_: Admin, organizer, owner

**Attendee**:
An elementary student or younger child who takes part in the activity.
_Avoid_: Participant, kid, audience

**Reply**:
All time blocks one classmate has saved for one event.
_Avoid_: Answer, response, response form

**Candidate day**:
A date proposed for the activity. Each becomes a column in the availability grid.
_Avoid_: Option, date

**Block**:
One half-hour window on a candidate day that a classmate marks as free.
_Avoid_: Slot, cell
